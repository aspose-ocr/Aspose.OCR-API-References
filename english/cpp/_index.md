The `aspose::ocr` namespace provides 23 structs covering input handling, recognition settings, preprocessing filters, progress reporting, and result representation, plus 42 global functions for page recognition, area detection, skew correction, and license management. It supports a wide range of image formats including JPEG, PNG, TIFF, and BMP.
