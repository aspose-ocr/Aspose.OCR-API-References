The library enables developers to integrate OCR processing into C++ applications, supporting recognition of printed and handwritten text across more than 100 languages. It handles common image formats such as JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, as well as PDF and multi‑page TIFF documents. All classes reside in the aspose::ocr namespace, offering a clear hierarchy for engine configuration, image loading, and result extraction.
